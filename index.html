MetaClean

Strip and edit photo metadata, privately, on your device.

Support

Questions, problems or feature requests? Open a support request and we'll get back to you.

How it works: choose a photo, tap Erase all metadata, then save or share the clean copy. Optionally add your own date taken, camera, author, copyright, description or location before saving. Your original photo is never changed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MetaClean processes your photos entirely on your device. We do not upload, store, or have access to your photos or their metadata.

  • Data we collect: none. MetaClean has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Photo access: used only to let you pick photos and save the cleaned copies you choose to save.
  • Purchases (if offered): handled by Apple / Google and our payment processor RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts to unlock features. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads (if shown): free versions may show non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, which may process device information as described in Google's privacy policy.

Children: MetaClean is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Contact us via the support link above.
